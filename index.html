About OBMC

A co-educational school that guides children through Dr. Maria Montessori's holistic approach, mixing time-tested methods with fresh ideas. It is a strong alternative to international schools.

Our mission

To help students grow into confident, self-driven people who can succeed, lead, and make a difference in a changing world.

Our campuses

OBMC Angeles

Angeles City, Pampanga

A lively Montessori community in Pampanga.

OBMC Fairview

Fairview, Quezon City

Quality Montessori learning for young families.

OBMC Greenhills

Greenhills, San Juan City

A creative, fun space that builds curiosity and service.

OBMC Las Piñas

B.F. Homes, Las Piñas City

A campus that celebrates the city's culture and history.

OBMC Sta. Ana

Sta. Ana, Manila City

A heritage-district school known for strong academics.
